Shoulder Prehab
Foam Roller Thoracic Extension
Targets Thoracic spine extension
How Roller horizontally across mid-back. Hands behind head to support neck. Extend backward over roller, hold 5 sec. Move up an inch, repeat. Cover mid-back to base of neck. Never below the bottom of the ribcage.
Dose 5–8 extensions per position, 3–4 positions. ~2–3 min total.
Avoid Rolling into the lower back. Stop at the bottom of the ribs.
Doorway Pec Stretch — Two Heights
Targets Pec major (lower arm), pec minor (higher arm)
How Stand in a doorway, forearm against the frame. Step through until you feel the stretch across the chest — not the shoulder joint. Version A: elbow at nipple height. Version B: elbow at shoulder height.
Dose 2 × 20 sec each position, each side.
Avoid Feeling it in the front of the shoulder — ease off. The corner variant (both arms on opposite walls, lean in) is often safer.
Knee Prehab
ATG Split Squat
Targets Quads, hip flexors, knee health
How Split stance, front foot flat. Slowly lower the back knee toward the floor, letting the front knee travel forward over — and ideally past — the toes. The goal is touching the knee to the ground with control. Use a wall or hold a support until you build the range.
Dose 2 × 10 per side. Go only as deep as you can control.
Avoid Collapsing the front arch or letting the heel rise. Weight stays on the whole front foot throughout the descent.
Backward Step-Up (Safety Step)
Targets Quad, VMO, knee stability
How Stand facing away from a low step (4–8 inches to start). Step one foot back onto the step. Press through that heel to stand up. Lower the free leg slowly back to the floor. Keep the working knee tracking over the toes throughout.
Dose 2 × 12 per side.
Avoid Letting the knee cave inward on the press. If it does, lower the step height.
Swim Session  ·  ~1,750 yds / 1,600 m
1
Warmup
Easy Free + Count Your Strokes
300 yds
Do 4 × 75 easy freestyle. Swim relaxed — this isn't a fitness set, it's just getting your body in the water.
Count On every length of the 3rd and 4th 75, count your strokes. One count = one arm entry. Write the number down — this is your baseline for everything that follows.
Rest 15 seconds between each 75.
Your stroke count matters. Whatever number you get on lengths 3 and 4 — that's your benchmark. Write it down. Every drill today is trying to make that number go down.
2
Drill 1 — Watch video first
Closed-Fist Freestyle
200 yds
Do 4 × 25 closed fists, 15 sec rest. Ball your hands into tight fists and swim normal freestyle.
Then 1 × 100 open-hand freestyle immediately after. Count your strokes on the last two lengths. Water should feel noticeably heavier in your hands.
Focus Forearm angled down, not just the elbow. Feel the water with your whole arm.
3
Focus Laps
Stroke Count Aerobic
400 yds
Do 4 × 100 at easy-moderate pace, 20 sec rest between each.
Rule Count strokes on every single length. Your cap = your warmup count. If you go over by 2+ strokes, slow down for the next 100.
Goal Not speed — holding the same stroke count across all 16 lengths. This is the whole game for distance swimming.
Why this matters: The closed-fist drill just taught your forearm what it's supposed to feel like. These 400 yards lock that in at full stroke. Count climbs = technique broke down — back off the pace.
4
Drill 2 — Watch video first
Wall Pull → Power Pulls
250 yds
Part A Wall pull — 3 × 10 sec at the wall. Hands and forearms flat on deck. Press straight down and back. Feel your body rise. Rest 10 sec between each. Teaches your arms what pulling backward actually means.
Part B 4 × 25 power pulls with pull buoy. Odd lengths: closed fists. Even lengths: open hands. 15 sec rest. Forearm presses straight back, not sweeping.
Part C 1 × 100 full freestyle. Count strokes. Did the number drop vs warmup? Even one fewer stroke per length is a win.
5
Main Set
Get to 1,600 meters
600 yds
Do 600 yards straight — or 2 × 300 with 30 sec rest if needed. Easy to moderate pace.
Focus Count strokes on the first and last length of every 100. Notice if your count creeps up as you fatigue. Don't fight it — just notice it.
Pace If you're gasping, slow down. Aerobic base + stroke under fatigue — those are the same thing.
Cooldown built in: Last 50 of the set — drop to real easy pace and try to match or beat your warmup stroke count. That's your feedback on whether today's drills stuck.

Done. 1,750 yards / ~1,600 meters.
